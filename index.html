About Us

A little about Methodius Media and how to reach us.

Methodius Media is an independent Catholic music label dedicated to creating sacred sound rooted in Byzantine and Eastern Catholic tradition. All music is released under CC0 — fully public domain, freely usable by the Church and her people.

We operate two imprints: Catholic Lofi, for ambient and contemplative music suited to prayer and study, and Chapel Pop, for upbeat contemporary music that carries the faith into everyday life.

Our work is born from a conviction that beauty, freely given, is itself an act of evangelization.
