About Us

A little about Methodius Media and how to reach us.

Methodius Media is an independent Catholic music label dedicated to creating sacred sound rooted in Byzantine and Latin Catholic tradition. All music is released under CC0 — fully public domain, freely usable by the Church and her people.

We operate two imprints: Catholic Lofi, for ambient and contemplative music suited to prayer and study, and Chapel Pop, for upbeat contemporary music for kids that carries the faith into everyday life.

Saints Cyril & Methodius inspire us to bridge the divide between Eastern and Western catechesis and to make the faith easier for everyone to understand. We currently produce lofi and pop music for adults and children. With your support and donations, we hope to expand our vision—bringing live-action and animated musicals to life, from shorts to feature films.
